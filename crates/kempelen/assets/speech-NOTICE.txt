Speech components of kempelen

Kokoro-82M-v1.0 ONNX model and voice data
Publisher: onnx-community; upstream model: hexgrad/Kokoro-82M.
The publisher model card declares Apache-2.0.
Source: https://huggingface.co/onnx-community/Kokoro-82M-v1.0-ONNX
The exact revision, file sizes and SHA-256 digests appear in manifest.json.
See notices/Kokoro-model-card.md and notices/Kokoro-LICENSE.txt.
Model and voice bytes are not modified by kempelen.

jespersen pronunciation library (built into kempelen)
Every narrated language but Chinese is pronounced by jespersen, a text-to-phonemes library
dedicated to the public domain (CC0 1.0). Source: https://github.com/Federiker/jespersen
Its language data comes from these sources, under their own licences:
- CMUdict (Carnegie Mellon University), for American English; its notice follows below.
- Moby Pronunciator II (Grady Ward), for English homographs: public domain.
- Danish and Swedish pronunciations from NST's lexicons, Språkbanken at the National Library of
  Norway (CC0).

Mandarin pronunciation (built into kempelen)
Chinese is pronounced by kempelen's own code, not by jespersen, following the front end
Kokoro's Chinese voices were trained with. It uses, as source ideas or data, and under their
own licences:
- misaki (Apache-2.0, hexgrad): the sound table and punctuation rules. Source:
  https://github.com/hexgrad/misaki
- pinyin-to-ipa (MIT, Stefan Taubert), which misaki adapts: the syllable-to-sound mapping.
- pypinyin (MIT, mozillazg) and the pinyin-data / phrase-pinyin-data it is built from (MIT):
  character and phrase readings in crates/kempelen/mandarin. Source: https://github.com/mozillazg/python-pinyin
- jieba (MIT, fxsjy) word dictionary, through the jieba-rs crate (MIT, messense).
- cn2an (MIT, Ailln): the rules for reading numbers in Chinese.
- OpenCC (Apache-2.0, BYVoid): the table that writes Traditional characters as Simplified ones before
  Taiwan and Hong Kong text is read (crates/kempelen/mandarin/TSCharacters.txt).
The data files are generated by crates/kempelen/mandarin/export.py and checked against misaki's own
output in the tests. Source: https://github.com/Federiker/kempelen

ONNX Runtime
The native runtime is supplied by the Cargo ort/ort-sys build, not fetched by
speech synthesis. Its notices come with the program's other third-party licences.
